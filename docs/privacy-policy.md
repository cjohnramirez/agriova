# Agriova privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026. Draft for review before the first store release._

Agriova is a farm record book for small farmers. This policy says what the app
keeps about you, why, who else sees it, and how to remove it.

## What we keep

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your email address | To send your sign-in code and recognise your account. |
| Your name and barangay | Shown in the app, and to set up your account on a new phone. |
| Your farm records: plots, plantings, expenses, harvests and sales | The purpose of the app: to show what your farm spends and earns. |
| Questions you ask the farm assistant | To answer them. |
| How many assistant questions you asked each day | To keep to the daily limit. |

Agriova does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It
has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Where it is kept

Your records are saved first on your phone, so the app works without signal.
When you have signal they are copied to your account on Supabase, which hosts
our database, so they are safe if you lose or change phones. Only you can read
your records; the database refuses anyone else's account.

## Who else sees it

- **Supabase** stores your account and records for us.
- **Google (Gemini)** receives each question you ask the farm assistant,
  together with a short summary of your farm records (crops, amounts and dates,
  but not your name or email), so the answer can use your own figures.
- **Mailjet** sends the sign-in code to your email address.
- **Open-Meteo** provides weather forecasts. The app asks for the forecast of
  the pilot area, not your own position, so nothing about you is sent.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for advertising.

## Removing it

In the app, go to **Settings → Delete my account**. This deletes your account
and every record on our server straight away, and clears the app on your
phone. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app, email us at the address below and we will delete
the account for you.

## Children

Agriova is made for adult farmers and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and important changes
are shown in the app.
