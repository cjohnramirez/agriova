## English

**Short description (Play, 80 max):**
Farm record book: know what your crops cost, earn and when to sell. Works offline.

**Subtitle (App Store, 30 max):** Know if your farm is earning

**Full description:**

Agriova is a farm record book made for small farmers in the Philippines.

Write down what you plant, what you spend, what you harvest and what you sell.
Agriova adds it up and shows whether this season is earning, crop by crop and
plot by plot.

- Works without signal. Records are saved on your phone and back up when you
  are online again.
- In Bisaya and English.
- Warns you before harvested produce spoils, and when the day is too hot for
  field work or rain is coming.
- A farm assistant answers questions about your own records: which crop earns
  most, when to sell, what you spent on fertilizer.
- Big buttons and short forms, made for use in the field.
- Change phones without losing your farm: sign in with your email.

Made for the pilot in Cagayan de Oro.
